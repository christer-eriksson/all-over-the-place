    How it works

    1. Choose an area with a rectangle or polygon.
    2. Load streets pulls every walkable way inside it from OpenStreetMap.
    3. Adjust the course. Click a street to run, avoid or block it. Avoided streets are covered once if needed but never repeated. Blocking a piece also drops anything it cuts off, such as the rest of a dead end. A cut makes a street impassable at one spot. You can also draw your own paths, whose ends snap to streets within 30 m, and pick a start.
    4. Plan the route. This solves the route inspection problem, also called the Chinese Postman Problem. It pairs up corners with an odd number of streets along the cheapest connections, runs those streets twice, and traces one continuous loop.
    5. Export a GPX or TCX file and import it as a course in Garmin Connect.

    While drawing, press Enter to finish, Backspace to undo a point and Esc to cancel. Everything you set is remembered in this browser. Your own paths and cuts are kept when you clear or move the area, and are used wherever they fall inside it.
