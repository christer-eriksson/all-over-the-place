    How it works

    1. Mark your turf with a rectangle or polygon.
    2. Fetch pulls every walkable way inside it from OpenStreetMap (via the Overpass API).
    3. Tune: click a street to choose Run it, Avoid (covered once if needed, never run twice) or Block (never run). Blocking a piece also drops anything it cuts off, such as the rest of a dead-end branch. Cut here makes a street impassable at one spot, so you can block just part of it. Toggle dead ends, draw your own shortcuts (ends snap to the nearest street within 30 m) and pick a start.
    4. GO! solves the Chinese Postman Problem (route inspection): it finds the shortest closed loop that covers every street at least once. Corners with an odd number of streets are paired up along the cheapest connections, those streets are run twice (orange), then an Euler circuit is traced.
    5. Download GPX and import it as a course in Garmin Connect, then send it to your watch.

    Keyboard while drawing: Enter finish · Backspace undo point · Esc cancel. Your area, exclusions and paths are remembered in this browser.
